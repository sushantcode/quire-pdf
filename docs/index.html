Quire PDF

Arrange, merge and compress PDFs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Getting help

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Say which device and version you're using, and what you expected to happen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move a page to another place?

On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold a page, then drag it onto the page whose place it should take. It can go anywhere, including among another file's pages. To move several, tap Select, choose the pages, and use the buttons at the bottom. On a Mac, drag the page; ⌘-click and Shift-click select several.

Are my original files changed?

No. Quire PDF only reads them. Everything you do changes only the merged file it creates.

Where does the merged PDF go?

On iPhone and iPad, choose Save to Files or Share on the result screen. On a Mac, you choose where to save before it merges.

What do the compression levels do?

  • None keeps everything exactly as it was.
  • Balanced shrinks images to JPEG at up to 150 dpi and keeps text selectable. On iPhone and iPad, pages with images may be saved as a picture with a hidden text layer. Their text stays selectable and searchable, but their links are not kept.
  • Maximum saves every page as a picture. It makes the smallest file, but text can no longer be selected.

If compressing would make the file bigger, Quire PDF saves it uncompressed instead.

Why can't I open a password-protected PDF?

Quire PDF does not open locked PDFs. Remove the password in the app that created it, then add it again.

Does Quire PDF upload my documents?

No. It has no servers and makes no network connections. See the privacy policy.
