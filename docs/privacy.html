Quire PDF

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026. Applies to Quire PDF for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Quire PDF does not collect, store, or share any information about you. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to send them somewhere.

Your documents

The PDFs you open are read, rearranged and merged entirely on your device. Quire PDF has no servers, and the app makes no network connections. Your original files are never modified.

  • Shared to the app: when you share a PDF to Quire PDF from another app, iOS may give it a copy of the file. Quire PDF deletes that copy as soon as it has read it.
  • Merged files: a merged PDF is kept in a temporary file, protected by your device's data protection, until you close its result screen. It is then deleted.
  • Sharing: when you use Share or Save to Files, the file goes only where you send it. What happens to it there is governed by that app or service.

What the app stores

The only thing Quire PDF remembers between uses is the compression level you last chose, stored on your device. It is not linked to you and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Quire PDF collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. A change that collects any information would only come with a new version of the app, and this page would say so first.
